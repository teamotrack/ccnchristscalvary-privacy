1. Overview

CCN Christ's Calvary Connect ("we", "our", or "us") provides a digital platform intended to help users stay connected with our church community and access content and services made available through our website and Android application (together, the "Services").

This Privacy Policy describes how information may be collected, used, disclosed, and protected when you access connect.ccnchristscalvary.com or use the Android app.

Please note: This policy must be reviewed and updated to match the actual features, permissions, hosting setup, and third-party tools used by the Services.

2. Information We Collect

The information handled by the Services depends on the features you use. We may process the following categories where applicable:

  • Information you provide: Information you voluntarily submit through contact forms, prayer requests, registrations, feedback, or other interactive features, if those features are available.
  • Technical information: Device type, operating system, app or browser version, language, and basic diagnostic information that may be generated when you access the Services.
  • Usage information: Pages or features accessed and interaction data, but only where analytics or similar measurement tools are enabled.
  • Location information: We do not intend to collect precise location information unless a feature requiring it is introduced and permission is requested.

Do not submit sensitive personal information unless it is specifically requested for a feature and you are comfortable providing it. Prayer requests or messages may contain personal or sensitive details; please share only what you choose to disclose.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used, where relevant, to:

  • Provide and maintain the website and Android app.
  • Display church updates, service information, broadcasts, radio content, and other resources.
  • Respond to enquiries, feedback, or prayer requests submitted by users.
  • Diagnose technical issues, maintain security, and improve the Services.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and protect the rights and safety of users and the organisation.

We do not use personal information for purposes materially different from those described here without providing appropriate notice or obtaining consent where required.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary:

  • With service providers who host, maintain, or support the Services and are expected to protect information they process.
  • With third-party content providers when you choose to access embedded or linked content.
  • When required by law, legal process, or a valid request from a competent authority.
  • To protect users, the public, or our legal rights where disclosure is necessary and permitted by law.

5. Third-Party Services and Content

The Services may link to or display content hosted by third parties, such as YouTube broadcasts, audio or radio streams, maps, social media, or external websites. When you interact with third-party content, those providers may collect information under their own privacy policies and terms.

We do not control the privacy practices of third-party services. Please review the relevant provider's privacy policy before using its services. For example, YouTube is operated by Google and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Only list services that are actually integrated into the current website or app. If analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or push-notification SDKs are used, identify them here and explain their data practices.

6. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Actual retention periods depend on the systems and services used by the organisation.

7. Children's Privacy

The Services are not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent where required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

8. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You may choose not to provide information requested through optional forms or features. You can also manage app permissions through your Android device settings.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have submitted, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your request and may retain certain information where required by law or for legitimate operational purposes.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our Services, practices, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Please review this page periodically.
